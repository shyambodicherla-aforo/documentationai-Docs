## Get Started

Create your initial project to start organizing docs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Dashboard">
    Log in at `https://aforo.com/dashboard`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a New Project">
    Click the **+ New Project** button in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Project">
    Name your project, add a short description (optional), then click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Team (Optional)">
    Add collaborators by entering their email addresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>
