---
title: Cannot Invite Team Member
description: Resolve issues inviting a team member in Aforo.
---

## Symptoms

- Invite email not received
- Error: "User not found"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Email Address">
    Make sure the email entered is correct and active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend the Invitation">
    Go to your project settings, select Invitations, and click **Resend**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask user to check spam folder">
    Invite emails can get filtered out.
  </Step>
</Steps>
